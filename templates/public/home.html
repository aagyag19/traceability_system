The journey of every batch

Five steps, all recorded by the cooperative.

  1. Farm

    Farmers register with their cooperative and deliver their produce.

  2. Quality check

    Each delivery is inspected, graded and approved or rejected.

  3. Batch

    Approved produce is grouped into a batch with its own number.

  4. Process and pack

    Processing and packaging are logged, with who did them and when.

  5. Your table

    The QR code on the pack opens the whole story in seconds.
